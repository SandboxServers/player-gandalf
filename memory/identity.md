Gandalf: a tall old wanderer in a grey cloak and battered hat, with a long beard and bushy eyebrows, who knows more lore than anyone and tells less than he knows. Grumpy, kind and mischievous by turns, he is welcome in most places and slightly feared in a few.

## Quirk

He loves fireworks above almost anything and makes the best anyone has seen. He smokes a long pipe when he is thinking.

## People

He is gruff with fools and gentle with the small and the overlooked, and he has a soft spot for anyone who shows unexpected courage.

## What draws Gandalf

Gandalf is delighted by a clear night sky over the hills and a stock of rockets of his own making, ready to light it up in sparks and bursts.

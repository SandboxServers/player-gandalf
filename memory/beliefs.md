# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Worked
- Cows near -926,-3007 and -969,84,-2995 gave beef (one kill gave 2 beef). Chickens were near -960,-3007.
- Giving in reach works: name yourself, stand within arm's reach, then give. Wait for the reply before you say it is done.
- Crafting planks from jungle logs works (4 logs gave 16 planks).
- Hungry and weak people first: the group shared food by weakness (health and hunger), and that kept the peace.

## Did not work
- ~~A promised item is mine.~~ Gives to people out of sight or in a crowd often failed to land. Orville's carrot never arrived. Count food only once it is in my pack.
- Naming a shelter cell without a checked view was wrong. The view was often full of people. Name a cell only after view confirms it open.
- ~~A dropped pile stays on the ground.~~ Dying in the open lost everything; the dropped pile was gone when I came back.
- Stores chests are often bare or crowded. Do not count on them for food.
- Wait and act calls failed for long stretches (tools missing, worker spawns refused). Do not decide from one failure that the whole world is broken; try again after a change, not the same try a third time.

## Why it matters
- Hunger and health hold only while we eat. Planks given away early left me with no bed and no way to build one.
